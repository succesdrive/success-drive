Road Accident Prevention Tips

Know Traffic Signs

Motorists should have knowledge of road traffic signs and how to interpret them.

Avoid Impaired Driving

Don't drive when fatigued, sleepy, under alcohol influence, or medically unfit.

Safe Overtaking

Avoid dangerous overtaking at bends, junctions, or on hills.

Safe Overtaking

Avoid dangerous overtaking at bends, junctions, or on hills.

Proper Lighting

Don't drive at night without functioning front and rear lights.

Speed Limits

Always obey posted speed limits for safer driving.

Tire Burst Response

If you have a flat tire, don't panic or brake suddenly. Hold steering firmly and slow down gradually.
