Know Your Traffic Signs

Understanding road signs is crucial for safe driving. Learn to recognize danger signs, regulatory signs, and informative signs to prevent accidents and obey traffic laws.

Danger Warning Signs

These signs warn of potential hazards or dangerous conditions ahead

Danger Warning

Dangerous Curve Ahead

Warns of a sharp curve to the left or right. Reduce speed before entering the curve.

Danger Warning

Deer Crossing

Indicates area where deer frequently cross the road. Be especially alert at dawn and dusk.

Danger Warning

Falling Rocks

Warns of potential rock slides or falling rocks, especially in mountainous areas.

Danger Warning

Slippery When Wet

Road surface becomes very slippery when wet. Reduce speed in rainy conditions.

Danger Warning

Pedestrian Crossing

Indicates area where pedestrians frequently cross. Be prepared to stop.

Danger Warning

Railroad Crossing

Warns of railroad tracks ahead. Always look both ways and never stop on tracks.
